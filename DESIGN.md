# Design System: LM · Esquadrias, Vidros e Construção

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "A Ficha Técnica"**

LM sells things that are measured, fabricated and installed — a window profile, a tempered pane, a pergola span. The site is built in that same grammar. Information is set the way a spec sheet is set: ruled rows, a label on the left, a value on the right, numbers in tabular figures. Nothing is presented as a claim when it can be presented as a measurement. The visitor is meant to read the company the way they'd read the section drawing of an aluminium frame.

Materially the world is two substances. **Paper** — a warm off-white with a second, slightly deeper tone under it — carries everything that must be read: catalogue, prose, tables, forms. **Ink** — a near-black warm brown — carries everything that must be felt: the hero, the closing call, the footer, the section that shows work. Photography lives only on ink or behind glass, never floating on paper. Gold is the third material and it is used like brass hardware: a 5px dot, a 2px underline, a filled marker, one button. It is never a surface.

Density is editorial, not dashboard. Bands run 56–88px of vertical air; prose is capped by a `ch` measure so a line never outruns the eye; headings carry hard negative tracking while uppercase labels open wide. The build refuses two things by construction: the institutional timeline page with cards of "values" (Sobre answers with a spec sheet instead), and the address-left/map-right business card (Localização answers with a numbered route to the door).

**Key Characteristics:**
- Two substances only — warm paper for reading, warm ink for feeling — alternating as full-bleed bands.
- Gold as hardware: dots, underlines, markers, one button. Never a field.
- Data set as ruled lists with tabular numerals, not as cards.
- Hairlines at rest; shadow and lift are responses to the pointer, never a resting state.
- Glass (blur + inset highlight) exists, but only over photography or ink.
- One serif moment and one animated moment per page. Both are ceilings.

## Colors

A warm neutral world — everything is pulled toward brown, nothing is pulled toward blue — with a single metallic accent standing in for brass and anodised finish.

### Primary
- **Ouro LM** (#c59a32): The accent and the only saturated colour in the system. It appears as the eyebrow dot and eyebrow text, the active-nav underline, the gold pill button, the leading dot on a capability row, the filled marker on the route's destination, the focus ring on a product card, and the focus glow on a field. Its budget is small on purpose: on a full band it should read as hardware, not as paint.
- **Ouro Claro** (#e6c76f): The gold that survives on ink. Used for the focus ring (2px, offset 3px) everywhere, for the "ficha" panel title, for the traced icons in the channel list, for the active count badge in the catalogue sidebar, and for the carousel progress bar. Rule of thumb: on paper use Ouro LM, on ink use Ouro Claro.
- **Creme** (#ead38a): The lighter button fill used when a gold action sits directly on hero photography and the deeper gold would sink into it.
- **Ouro Tinta** (#9d7521): Gold as *text* on a light surface — the product badge over a photo, the mobile filter-sheet legend, the "limpar filtros" control. This is the only gold that is ever read as a word on paper.

### Neutral
- **Tinta** (#1c1b18): The dark substance. Page background behind everything (`html`), the closing bands, the contact card, the footer, the solid header, the active state of every filter control, and the text colour for all body copy on paper.
- **Tinta Profunda** (#191816): A single step darker, used only for the category band so it reads as a recess between two paper bands.
- **Papel** (#f8f5ef): The reading surface. The default section background and the modal panel.
- **Papel 2** (#f2ede3): The second paper. Its whole job is to separate two consecutive paper bands without going dark — the história band on Sobre, the visita band on Localização, the contact form.
- **Cartão** (#fbf8f2): The card surface, a half-step above paper so a card lifts off its band without a shadow.
- **Campo** (#fbf9f4): The input surface — the lightest tone in the system. Fields read as cut into the paper, not laid on it.
- **Filete** (#ded8cc): The hairline. Every divider, every card ring, every table rule.
- **Cinza Texto** (#6f6b63): Secondary prose — leads, descriptions, captions, the muted half of a label/value pair.

### Named Rules
**The Hardware Rule.** Gold is hardware, not paint. It may be a dot, a rule, a marker, an icon, an underline or one button — it may not be a background wider than a button. If a comp has a gold panel, the panel is wrong.

**The Two Substances Rule.** A surface is paper or it is ink. There is no third background colour and no gradient between them. Paper 2 and Tinta Profunda are not new substances; they are the same two materials one step apart, used only to keep two same-substance bands from fusing.

**The Warm Neutral Rule.** Every neutral in this system is warm. If a new value reads cool or grey next to `#f8f5ef`, it is not from this world — including Tailwind's `neutral-*` scale, which is the legacy register (see Do's and Don'ts).

## Typography

**Display / Body / UI Font:** Space Grotesk (variable, with `sans-serif` fallback), loaded as `--font-space-grotesk`
**Accent Font:** Instrument Serif 400 (with `serif` fallback), loaded as `--font-instrument-serif`

**Character:** Space Grotesk does all the work — it is technical enough to set a spec table and humane enough to set a lead paragraph, and its wide weight range (400 through 750, all real on the variable face) makes a whole hierarchy possible in one family. Instrument Serif appears exactly once per page at most, and currently in exactly one place in the whole build: the year on the história band. It is the system's only ornament, and its scarcity is what makes it register.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (600 nominal; 540–650 in practice, `clamp(38px, 4vw, 60px)`, line-height 1.02–1.06, tracking -.045em): Page `h1` only, one per surface. Always `text-wrap: balance` and always capped at 15–16ch so it breaks into two or three deliberate lines. Home pins 48–60px; catálogo 56px; sobre and localização clamp from 38–40px up.
- **Display Serif** (400, `clamp(62px, 7vw, 92px)`, line-height .88, tracking -.03em): The single ornamental number. One per page, maximum — currently the founding year.
- **Headline** (600, `clamp(30px, 3vw, 44px)`, line-height 1.05–1.08, tracking -.04em): Band titles (`h2`). The older home bands pin fixed sizes (44 / 42 / 40px) and step down to 38px at 800px and 34px at 520px; new surfaces use the clamp and reach 30px at 520px. Capped at 14–17ch.
- **Title** (650, 19–21px, line-height 1.1–1.25, tracking -.025 to -.028em): Card names, capability rows, list-item headings.
- **Body** (400, 15–17px, line-height 1.6–1.68): Leads and prose, in Cinza Texto on paper or white-at-70% on ink. Always capped by measure — 52ch for a closing paragraph, 58ch for a lead, 60–68ch for long prose.
- **Body Small** (400, 12–14px, line-height 1.42–1.56): Card descriptions, notes, footnotes under a form.
- **Label** (700, 11–12px, tracking .11–.14em, uppercase): Eyebrows, `dt` in every data sheet, sidebar group legends, footer group titles.
- **Micro** (700–750, 9–10px, tracking .08–.13em, uppercase): Card footers, result counts, badges, filter-state captions. The smallest type in the system; never used for anything a visitor must read to act.

### Named Rules
**The Tracking Follows Size Rule.** Tracking is a function of size and runs monotonically: -.045em at display, -.04em at headline, -.025em at title, normal at body, then *positive* — .08em to .2em — the moment type goes uppercase and under 12px. A 44px heading at normal tracking, or an 11px uppercase label at normal tracking, are both immediately wrong.

**The Measure Rule.** Every text block declares a cap in `ch`, not in pixels: 14–17ch for headings, 52–68ch for prose. A paragraph without a measure cap is unfinished.

**The One Serif Rule.** Instrument Serif appears at most once per page, on a number or a date. It never sets a heading, never sets prose, and never appears twice on one surface.

**The Balance/Pretty Rule.** Headings take `text-wrap: balance`; prose takes `text-wrap: pretty`. Both are applied on every heading and paragraph in the system, without exception.

## Layout

**Container.** One container, everywhere: `.lm-container` is `width: min(calc(100% - 48px), 1216px)` centred — a 1216px grid with 24px gutters, dropping to 20px gutters (`--lm-mobile-gutter`) below 800px. Bands are full-bleed; only their content is contained. There is no second max-width nested inside a container anywhere in the build.

**Band stack.** Every page is a vertical stack of full-bleed bands, each with its own substance, alternating so no two adjacent bands share a background. Home: ink hero → paper catálogo → deep-ink categories → paper linhas → photographic processo → paper contato → ink footer. Sobre: ink sheet → photographic band → paper capacidades → paper-2 história → paper equipe → ink CTA. Localização: paper topo/rota → mapa em faixa cheia → paper-2 visita → ink canais.

**Vertical rhythm.** Desktop band padding runs 84–88px on the newer surfaces, 76px on Localização, 64px on the home bands. Every one of them collapses to 52–56px below 800px. Inside a band the ladder is 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 24 / 32 / 40 / 48 / 64: eyebrow → 15–22px → heading → 16–22px → lead → 24–28px → action; a ruled list's rows sit at 24–28px of padding with a 1px rule between.

**Column grammar.** Two columns, deliberately unequal, `align-items: start`, gap 48–72px: a reading column at `minmax(0, 1fr)` and an evidence column at a fixed-ish `minmax(0, 430px)`. Grids: 4 columns for home products and categories, 3 for the catalogue and the team roster, 250px sidebar + `minmax(0, 1fr)` for the catalogue layout. Where the evidence is a single artefact that has to be read on its own — the Localização map — it does not become a parallel column: it drops out of the container into a full-bleed band beneath the passage it belongs to, and the reading column stays capped (`.local-topo__inner` at 860px, the roteiro at 760px).

**Sticky offsets.** Sticky elements park at `top: 92px` (the catalogue sidebar); anchor targets carry `scroll-margin-top` of 96px on desktop, 80–72px on mobile — matched to the 68px/64px header plus air.

**Breakpoints.** Three, and only three: **1100px** (columns tighten, 4-up becomes 2-up, the header drops its tagline), **800px** (everything goes single column; grids become horizontal snap carousels; the nav becomes a blurred dropdown behind a 44px toggle; the catalogue sidebar becomes a bottom sheet; the detail modal becomes a full-screen slide-in), **520px** (label/value pairs stack vertically, buttons go full-width, carousel cards go edge-to-edge). Two behavioural queries sit alongside them: `@media (hover: none)` removes every hover transform, and the reduced-motion pair described under Components → Motion.

### Named Rules
**The Band Rule.** A page is a stack of full-bleed bands, each one substance, and the container never carries a background of its own. If you need a colour change, you need a new band.

**The Closing Ink Rule.** Every page ends on an ink band carrying the primary action, immediately above the ink footer. Sobre closes on `sobre-cta`, Localização on `local-canais`, Home on the contact band. A new surface that ends on paper is unfinished.

**The No Empty Half Rule.** When a two-column band loses its evidence half — no map, no photo, no image in the CMS — the band collapses to one column and the reading column widens (the `is-solo` modifier). It never ships with half a section blank. This is a layout obligation, not a nicety: content is CMS-driven and the empty case is the normal case.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is **flat on paper and glassy on ink**. Nothing on a paper band casts a shadow at rest — separation is done with a hairline (`1px solid #ded8cc` as a border, or `inset 0 0 0 1px` as a ring that costs no layout) and with a half-step of tone (`#fbf8f2` card on `#f8f5ef` paper). Shadow is a *response*: it appears when the pointer arrives and disappears with it, and `@media (hover: none)` removes it entirely.

On ink and over photography the depth model changes completely: panels are built from a translucent gradient fill, a `backdrop-filter` blur of 16–28px, an inset top highlight and an inset bottom shade. This is the only place real material thickness is expressed, and it is what makes the header, the hero feature tiles and the processo cards read as glass over a photograph rather than as boxes on a page.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Hairline ring** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px #ded8cc, 0 1px 2px rgb(28 25 20 / 3%)`): The resting state of every card on paper. The 3% shadow is a seat, not a lift.
- **Card lift** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px rgb(155 119 37 / 38%), 0 18px 42px rgb(39 31 21 / 12%)` with `transform: translateY(-5px)`): Hover on a catalogue card. Note the ring warms to gold as it lifts.
- **Tile lift** (`box-shadow: 0 20px 45px rgb(0 0 0 / 38%)` with `transform: translateY(-6px) scale(1.035)`): Hover on a dark photographic tile.
- **Glass panel** (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px rgb(255 255 255 / 28%), inset 0 -1px rgb(255 255 255 / 8%), 0 0 0 1px rgb(255 255 255 / 34%), 0 18px 40px rgb(0 0 0 / 18%)` + `backdrop-filter: blur(18px)`): Cards over photography.
- **Glass chrome** (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px rgb(255 255 255 / 18%), inset 0 -1px rgb(255 255 255 / 10%), 0 12px 34px rgb(10 7 5 / 18%)` + `backdrop-filter: blur(28px)`): The header over the home hero.
- **Ink panel ring** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 1px rgb(255 255 255 / 13%)` over a `rgb(255 255 255 / 4–5%)` fill): The data sheet and the channel rows. The ink equivalent of the hairline ring — no shadow, just a lit edge.
- **Modal** (`box-shadow: 0 34px 100px rgb(0 0 0 / 38%)`): The detail panel, the only element in the system allowed a shadow that large.
- **Bottom sheet** (`box-shadow: 0 -18px 60px rgb(0 0 0 / 24%)`): Upward, because the sheet comes from below.

### Named Rules
**The Hairline-At-Rest Rule.** On paper, a surface at rest is separated by a 1px line and a half-step of tone. Shadow is reserved for pointer response and for things that genuinely float (modal, sheet). A resting drop shadow on a paper card is out of world.

**The Glass Needs Ground Rule.** `backdrop-filter` is only legitimate where there is something to blur: over photography or over ink. Glass on a paper band is a defect, not a variant.

## Shapes

Radius is a function of surface area, and it climbs a clear ladder: **pill** (999px) for anything that holds a word — buttons, filter chips, tags, badges, the carousel progress bar; **circle** (50%) for anything that holds a mark — arrow discs (30/32/34/42px), the route markers (44px), the mobile toggle, the modal close (48px), the eyebrow dot (5px) and the capability dot (7px); **9–14px** for controls and small objects — inputs (9px), search fields and small cards (12px), avatars and the filter-state block (14px); **16px** for cards and rows; **20–24px** for media and panels — the data sheet (20px), the história image and the map (20–24px); **28–40px** for the largest objects — the contact card and form (34px, stepping to 28px then 24px), the modal (32px), the bottom sheet (32px on the top corners only), the hero images (38–40px).

Corners are always symmetric except where an object meets an edge: the bottom sheet rounds only its top corners, and the catalogue hero image squares its bottom corners when it becomes a full-width mobile band.

The declared `--lm-radius-sm / -md / -lg` (8 / 16 / 24px) custom properties describe this ladder's spine, but the built rules write the pixel value directly; treat the ladder above as normative and the tokens as its shorthand.

Two recurring geometries define the form language beyond radius. The **ruled row**: a `1px solid` top border, a leading marker in a fixed-width column (a 7px gold dot, a 44px numbered disc, a 56px avatar), and content in `minmax(0, 1fr)`. And the **spine**: a 1px vertical line running behind a column of circular markers, connecting them — drawn on Localização's route, and hidden by `:has(li:only-child)` when there is only one step to connect.

### Named Rules
**The Radius Follows Area Rule.** A 16px radius on a 48px control, or a 9px radius on a 500px panel, both read as mistakes. Pick from the ladder by the size of the thing.

**The Pill For Words Rule.** Anything that contains a word and can be pressed is a pill (999px). Anything that contains only a mark is a circle. There is no in-between rounded rectangle for actions.

## Components

### Buttons
The system's one action primitive. Every button and every button-shaped link in the design-system surfaces is `.button` plus a variant; there are no bespoke buttons.

- **Shape:** Full pill (999px), minimum height 44px (48px in the mobile hero, 50–54px on the newer surfaces and in the filter sheet), horizontal padding 22px (24px for the header CTA, 26–32px for a page's primary action), 12px gap between label and arrow.
- **Type:** 14px / weight 650 / line-height 1, never uppercase.
- **Gold** (`--gold`): the primary action on paper and on ink. **Creme**: the primary action laid directly over hero photography, where the deeper gold sinks into the image. **Escuro**: the secondary action, ink fill with a `rgb(255 255 255 / 18%)` border so it survives on both substances.
- **States:** Hover shifts the fill one step lighter over 180ms. `:active` presses to `scale(.96)` over 100ms. Focus draws a 2px `Ouro Claro` outline at 3px offset.
- **Content:** Label plus an optional trailing arrow. Buttons open in a `.button-row` (flex, 12px gap, wrapping) which goes full-width column below 520px.

### Eyebrow
A 5px gold dot and 11px uppercase gold label at .2em tracking, 9px apart, opening a band above its heading. Present on every design-system surface. Use it where the band genuinely carries a second, categorising label — not as an automatic section preamble.

### Arrow Disc
The system's recurring affordance: a circular hairline-ringed disc (30–42px) holding an arrow, sitting at the corner of a card or the end of a row. At rest it is a translucent ring; on parent hover it inverts to near-white fill with ink glyph and nudges in the direction it points (`translateX(2px)`, or `rotate(-45deg) translate(1px, -1px)` for the diagonal variant). The whole disc is decorative and `aria-hidden`; the parent link carries the accessible name.

### Cards
- **Catalogue product card** — the canonical card. Cartão fill, 16px radius, hairline ring, minimum 476px tall with fixed-height zones (210px image, 48px title, 58px description, 48px tags, 40px footer) so a row of cards aligns regardless of copy length. A gold-ink badge floats top-left over the image on a 91%-opaque paper pill with an 8px backdrop blur. Hover: ring warms to gold at 38%, card lifts 5px, image scales 1.035 over 400ms. Focus: 3px `Ouro LM` outline at 4px offset. It is a `<button>`, and it opens a modal rather than navigating, so the catalogue's filters and scroll position survive.
- **Dark photographic tile** — 16px radius, translucent white outline, a bottom-up black gradient scrim to 72%, a 13px label bottom-left and an arrow disc bottom-right. Lifts 6px and scales 1.035 on hover; image scales 1.075.
- **Glass card** — over photography or ink only. Diagonal translucent gradient, 18px backdrop blur, four-layer shadow, a large tabular number and an arrow disc in the header row, then a hairline rule, then title and copy.

### Chips and Tags
- **Tag** (read-only): pill, `#eee8dc` fill with an inset `#dfd7c8` ring, 9–11px, muted brown text. Used for product attributes.
- **Filter chip** (interactive): pill, transparent with a `Filete` border and muted text at rest; the active state inverts to ink fill / white text. Minimum 40px desktop, 48px in the mobile sheet. The active state is *always* a fill inversion, never a colour-only change.
- **Count badge**: a pill of at least 24×22px with tabular numerals, muted on `#eee8de`, flipping to ink-on-`Ouro Claro` when its row is active.

### Inputs
- **Form field:** 45px tall (48px below 800px), 9px radius, `Campo` fill, 1px `Filete` border, 17px horizontal padding, 14px type (16px on mobile, to stop iOS from zooming). Its label sits above as an 11px uppercase tracked label in `#777168`.
- **Search field:** 12px radius, `Campo` fill, an inset 1px ring instead of a border, and a 19px leading icon column.
- **Focus:** the border (or ring) becomes `Ouro LM` and a 3px gold glow at 11–12% opacity blooms outside it. This is the only place gold is used as a diffuse surface, and it is 12% opaque.
- **Disabled:** `opacity: .42` and `cursor: default`; never a colour swap.

### Navigation
Two header shells for one nav. Over the home hero it is **fixed glass** — 62%-opaque warm ink, a 28px backdrop blur, a diagonal light sweep and a lit top edge. Everywhere else it is **solid sticky** — 96%-opaque ink with a 12px blur. Both are 68px tall (64px below 800px).

Links are 14px / weight 500 at 76% white, going to 100% on hover over 180ms. The active page is marked with `aria-current="page"` and rendered as full-white text plus a 2px `Ouro LM` underline at 7px offset — a shape change, not only a colour change. A gold pill CTA closes the row.

Below 800px the nav collapses behind a 44px circular toggle whose two bars cross into an X on `aria-expanded`, and opens as an 18px-radius blurred dropdown panel with 44px-tall link rows and the CTA at the bottom.

### Data Sheet (signature)
The component the whole world is named after. On ink: a 20px-radius panel with a 4%-white fill and a 13%-white inset ring, opened by an `Ouro Claro` uppercase title, then a `<dl>` of rows separated by 10%-white rules — `dt` an 11px uppercase tracked label at 58% white, `dd` 14px / 500 white, right-aligned, in tabular numerals. Below 520px each row stacks and the value left-aligns.

The same grammar recurs on paper without the panel: a `<dl>` or list of rows separated by `1px solid var(--lm-line)`, label left, tabular value right. Use it for opening hours, capabilities, specifications — anything with a label and a value.

### Route Spine (signature)
Localização's arrival sequence: a vertical 1px `Filete` line behind a column of 44px circular markers, each holding either a number in tabular figures or a traced icon. Intermediate markers are paper with an inset ring; the destination marker is filled `Ouro LM` with no ring. Each row carries a 12px uppercase heading, a `clamp(19px, 2vw, 23px)` value (larger at the destination), and an optional 52ch note. The connecting line is suppressed when only one step exists.

### Channel Row
The contact affordance on ink: a `44px / minmax(0, 1fr) / 34px` grid — a 26px `Ouro Claro` traced icon, a 16px label over an 11px uppercase caption, and an arrow disc — on a 16px-radius panel with a 5%-white fill and a 13%-white ring. Hover raises the fill to 9% and the ring to 28% and inverts the arrow disc.

### Icons
Traced SVG on a 24 viewBox, `fill: none`, `stroke: currentColor`, `stroke-width: 1.7`, round caps and joins (the mobile filter trigger runs 1.8 at 24px). Rendered at 18–26px. Every icon is `aria-hidden`; its meaning lives in the surrounding text.

### Overlays
- **Detail modal:** a `min(1120px, 100% - 64px)` × `min(760px, 100svh - 64px)` panel at 32px radius, split into an ink media column and a paper reading column, over a 68%-opaque ink backdrop with an 8px blur. It fades and rises 18px into place over 220–260ms. Below 800px it becomes a full-screen panel sliding in from the right with a sticky glass header, and the backdrop is dropped.
- **Bottom sheet:** below 800px only. Top corners at 32px, a 54px drag handle, a 30px title, up to `min(86svh, 720px)` tall, respecting `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`, sliding up over 240ms.

### Motion
- **Durations:** 100ms press · 150–180ms colour and state · 200–260ms lift, panel and sheet · 400–500ms image scale · 620–880ms entrance.
- **Easings:** `ease` for colour; `cubic-bezier(.2, .65, .3, 1)` for lift, carousel and sheet; `cubic-bezier(.2, .7, .3, 1)` and `cubic-bezier(.2, .75, .3, 1)` for cards and the modal; `cubic-bezier(.16, .84, .44, 1)` for entrances.
- **Transform budget:** hover lifts are `translateY(-4px)` to `translateY(-6px)` with scale ≤ 1.035; image scale inside a frame is ≤ 1.075.

**The Press Rule.** Every pressable thing scales to `.96` on `:active` over 100ms — buttons, filter chips, cards, thumbnails, sheet controls. It is the system's single tactile signature and it is applied without exception.

**The One Animated Moment Rule.** A page gets one entrance animation and no more. Sobre's is the spec sheet filling in row by row; Localização's is the route line drawing downward. Both are `lm-sobe` (14px rise + fade, 620ms) staggered in 80ms steps to a 480ms ceiling, and both live inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)` — opt-in, so motion is never the default. A global `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` block additionally clamps every animation and transition in the build to .01ms and disables smooth scrolling.

**The Hover Is A Bonus Rule.** `@media (hover: none)` strips every hover transform and shadow. Any state that only exists on hover must have a rest state that already reads — which is why active filters invert their fill, badges are always visible, and arrow discs are drawn at rest.

### Accessibility floors met by the build
These are the floors the shipped code actually holds; treat them as the minimum for a new surface, not as aspirations.
- **Focus is always visible**: 2px `Ouro Claro` at 3px offset on buttons, text links, header links and channel rows; 3px `Ouro LM` at 4px offset on catalogue cards; 2px at 4px offset on draggable carousels.
- **Hit targets ≥ 44px**: `.button`, `.text-link`, the mobile toggle, mobile nav rows, the modal close (48px); mobile chips run 48px and sheet actions 54px.
- **16px inputs on mobile**, so iOS does not zoom on focus.
- **State is never colour alone**: the active nav page adds an underline, active filters invert their fill, the destination marker changes fill and loses its ring.
- **Decorative marks are `aria-hidden`** (eyebrow dots, arrows, icons, dividers) and decorative images carry `alt=""` inside a wrapper with `role="img"` and an `aria-label`.
- **`lang="pt-BR"`** on the document; all copy, labels and admin vocabulary in pt-BR.
- **Tabular numerals** on every figure a reader compares down a column.
- **Scroll containers** carry `overscroll-behavior` containment, and anchor targets carry `scroll-margin-top` matched to the sticky header.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** compose a new page as a stack of full-bleed bands, alternating substance, and end it on an ink band carrying the primary action.
- **Do** wrap every band's content in `.lm-container` and let the band's background run full-bleed. One container, no nested max-widths.
- **Do** cap every text block with a `ch` measure — 14–17ch headings, 52–68ch prose — and set `text-wrap: balance` on headings, `pretty` on paragraphs.
- **Do** set anything with a label and a value as a ruled list with tabular numerals: `dt` uppercase and muted, `dd` full-contrast and right-aligned, rows divided by `1px solid var(--lm-line)`.
- **Do** use `.button` plus a variant for every action, and pick the variant by ground: gold on paper or ink, creme over photography, dark for the secondary.
- **Do** draw new icons as 24-box SVG at `stroke-width: 1.7` with round caps and joins, `fill: none`, `stroke: currentColor`.
- **Do** give a two-column band an `is-solo` collapse for when its evidence half has no CMS content.
- **Do** hold hover lift within `translateY(-4px … -6px)` and scale ≤ 1.035, and make sure the rest state still reads when `@media (hover: none)` strips it.
- **Do** put every entrance animation behind `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`, and keep it to one moment per page.
- **Do** give every new interactive element a ≥44px target and the `Ouro Claro` focus ring.

### Don't:
- **Don't** introduce a colour outside the palette above. If a comp needs a new hue, it needs a new argument first.
- **Don't** fill a surface with gold. Gold is a dot, a rule, a marker, an icon or one button; the only diffuse gold in the system is a 12%-opacity focus glow.
- **Don't** write a new surface in Tailwind's `neutral-*` utilities. `/solucoes`, `/trabalhos` and `/catalogo/[slug]` are still in that generic register (`max-w-6xl`, `text-neutral-600`, `border-neutral-300`) and read as a different product — that is **legacy drift to be migrated onto the design system, not a second sanctioned style**. The design system is `globals.css`; the surfaces that speak it are Home, Catálogo, Sobre and Localização. (The `bg-white text-neutral-900` on `<body>` in the frontend layout is the same holdover.)
- **Don't** put glass on a paper band, or a resting drop shadow under a paper card. On paper, rest is a hairline.
- **Don't** reach for Instrument Serif more than once on a page, and never for a heading or for prose.
- **Don't** add a fourth breakpoint. The system is 1100 / 800 / 520 plus the two behavioural queries; a new surface that needs a fifth column count is over-specified.
- **Don't** add an eyebrow to a band that already carries a categorising label. It is an existing device in this build, not a required section-opener — a heading that stands alone is finished.
- **Don't** signal state with colour alone; add a fill inversion, an underline or a shape change.
- **Don't** reference a custom property that isn't declared in `:root`. The 800px block currently calls `var(--lm-gray-600)` and `var(--lm-gold-500)`, neither of which exists — the mobile search clear button therefore loses its colour and its focus ring. That is a bug in the build, not a token; new code must not copy it.
- **Don't** let a card's height float with its copy in a grid. The catalogue card fixes each zone's height so a row aligns; a new card in a grid must do the same.
